## Motion, responsiveness and accessibility

Implement four purposeful motion scenes: layered hero entry; explanatory airflow layers; image-mask reveal for manufacturing/certification media; per-card stagger plus restrained hover/touch feedback. Every major content section and every repeated card must have a perceptible viewport entry in normal motion mode. A section wrapper animation does not substitute for each card.

Critical content must be visible in base CSS. Only apply temporary hidden states after a synchronous `data-motion-ready` enhancement marker. A failure fallback may restore visibility but must not consume future viewport animation. `prefers-reduced-motion`, no JS, low-power and failed WebGL must leave all content immediately readable and operable.

Desktop and 390px mobile need independent compositions, not simple shrinking. Keep product structures complete—no stretching or hard cropping of key parts. Use responsive image sizing, reserved aspect ratios, keyboard focus, 44px touch targets, WCAG AA contrast, semantic controls, Escape/lightbox focus return where relevant, and no horizontal overflow.
